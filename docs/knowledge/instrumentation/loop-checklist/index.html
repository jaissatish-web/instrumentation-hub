Loop Check Checklist

Comprehensive loop check checklist for pre-commissioning verification — signal, alarms, valve stroke, configuration and P&ID compliance.

CommissioningQA / QC

Contents

🎣The Hook

A control valve refused to respond at NSRP startup. The loop check revealed a reverse-configured AO card — caught on paper before energizing.

🧠Core Concept

  • Signal Verification: 4–20 mA loop check with a 250 Ω resistor or loop calibrator.
  • Alarm Check: Verify LL, L, H, HH setpoints in the DCS against the approved list.
  • Valve Stroke: Manual 0–100% stroke with feedback verification.
  • Configuration: Check tag numbers, engineering units, and action (direct/reverse).
  • P&ID Compliance: Cross-verify installation against loop diagrams and hook-ups.

🏭Real-World Story

ADNOC fired heater lost fuel gas during commissioning. Investigation showed control valve XV-201’s AO card was configured for reverse action. The configuration was corrected before automatic-mode entry — a loop check that paid for itself instantly.

🔑Takeaway

Loop checks catch configuration errors before startup — never skip the paper verification step.
